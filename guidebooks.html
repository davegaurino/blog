Guidebook

A collection of practical guidebooks designed to make useful ideas easier to understand and apply in everyday life.

Latest Guidebooks

Here are recent sample products in this category.

The Simple Productivity Guide

A practical guide for people who want to become more productive without filling every hour with work. It explains how to choose priorities, organize tasks, reduce distractions, and create routines that can be maintained over time. The guide focuses on simple methods rather than complicated productivity systems. It also includes suggestions for planning the day, reviewing progress, protecting rest time, and avoiding the habit of confusing busyness with real accomplishment. The goal is to help readers use their time more intentionally while still leaving room for relationships, health, reflection, and activities that make life meaningful.

Starting Your Personal Website

This guidebook walks beginners through the process of creating a simple personal website using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and GitHub Pages. It explains how pages connect, how folders and file names work, how to add images and navigation, and how to publish the finished site online. The material avoids unnecessary technical language and focuses on the parts a beginner actually needs to understand. Examples cover personal branding, portfolio pages, blogs, and product sections. The guide is intended for people who want control over their own website without immediately depending on WordPress, paid hosting, or a complicated development setup.

Better Digital Habits

A practical guide to building healthier habits around phones, social media, email, online entertainment, and daily screen use. Instead of treating technology as something that must be completely avoided, the guide helps readers decide what deserves their attention and what should be limited. It discusses notifications, distraction, bedtime habits, focused work, family time, and intentional use of social platforms. Simple exercises help readers identify the digital behaviors that waste time or increase stress. The aim is to create a healthier relationship with technology so it remains a useful tool rather than something that quietly controls the structure of the day.

A Practical Guide to Personal Growth

This guidebook brings together simple ideas for improving habits, decision-making, relationships, confidence, discipline, and personal responsibility. Each chapter focuses on one area that can be improved through small, repeated actions rather than dramatic changes. Readers are encouraged to examine what is already working, identify weak areas honestly, and make realistic adjustments they can sustain. The guide also discusses learning from mistakes, receiving feedback, setting boundaries, and avoiding comparison with other people. Its main purpose is to make personal development more practical by connecting improvement with everyday choices instead of treating growth as a collection of motivational ideas.

Faith in Everyday Decisions

A guide for readers who want their faith to influence ordinary decisions involving work, money, relationships, goals, time, and personal priorities. It uses biblical principles to show how prayer, Scripture, wisdom, conscience, and responsible planning can work together when a decision is not immediately clear. The guide does not promise that every choice will come with a dramatic sign. Instead, it encourages readers to develop habits that make thoughtful and faithful decisions more natural. Practical questions at the end of each section help readers examine motives, possible consequences, responsibilities, and whether a choice is consistent with the values they want to live by.
