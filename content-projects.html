Content Projects

Recent writing, educational media, digital publishing, and communication projects developed for practical audiences.

Latest Content Projects

Here are recent sample projects in this category.

Everyday Technology Explainer Series

A short-form content series that explains common technology topics using language that ordinary readers can understand. Each article begins with a practical situation, introduces the idea, and then explains how it affects everyday use. Topics can include online safety, cloud storage, artificial intelligence, website basics, and digital privacy. The project avoids unnecessary technical terms unless they are explained clearly. Visual examples and short summaries can be added for readers who prefer to scan. The purpose is to make technology less intimidating and more useful, especially for people who use digital tools regularly but do not have a technical background.

Safe Food at Home Guide

An educational content project focused on helping household cooks prevent food contamination, diarrhea, and food poisoning. The material follows food from buying and storage to preparation, cooking, serving, and keeping leftovers. Instead of relying only on warnings, the guide explains simple actions people can practice at home, such as separating raw and cooked food, keeping hands and utensils clean, and storing food properly. The writing is designed for everyday readers and can be adapted into a booklet, poster, or infographic series. The project combines practical communication with scientifically accurate information for household use.

Student Media Literacy Lessons

A set of learning materials designed to help students evaluate online information, identify misleading content, understand sources, and think more carefully before sharing posts. The lessons use familiar examples such as social media claims, edited images, viral headlines, and online advertisements. Activities encourage students to compare sources and explain why they trust or question particular information. The content is written in a way that can be used in classroom discussion, worksheets, or multimedia presentations. The project aims to develop practical media habits rather than simply teaching definitions, making the lessons easier to connect with students’ daily online experiences.

Personal Development Article Collection

A collection of practical articles about productivity, personal growth, relationships, purpose, faith, and everyday decision-making. Each article develops one topic through clear explanations, examples, and action-oriented suggestions rather than relying on short motivational statements. The writing is intended for general readers who want useful ideas they can apply in normal life. Topics can be expanded into blog posts, ebooks, email content, or downloadable guides. The project also serves as a long-term content library, allowing related articles to be grouped into themes and reused across different formats while keeping a consistent personal writing voice.

Digital Learning Resource Pack

A multimedia learning resource pack that combines short written explanations, presentation slides, activity sheets, and visual materials around one lesson topic. The project is designed so teachers can use the materials together or select only the parts that fit their class. Content is organized in a clear teaching sequence, beginning with background information and moving toward examples, student activities, and review. The design avoids placing too much information on one page or slide. This project demonstrates how written content and visual design can work together to support classroom instruction rather than treating multimedia as decoration alone.
