About Me

Educator, and technology professional sharing ideas, projects, writing, and digital work.

Hello, I’m Dave.

I’m Dave Gaurino, an educator, and technology professional with years of experience in teaching, web design, graphic design, multimedia, and IT support. I have worked as a computer teacher in the Philippines and Thailand, handling subjects related to information technology, digital media, and communication.

I hold a degree in Computer Science and a teaching diploma, and I am also a licensed professional teacher. I am currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Development Communication at the University of the Philippines Open University.

This website is a place where I share my ideas, projects, writing, digital work, and other things I continue to learn and create.
